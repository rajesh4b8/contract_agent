COMMERCIAL PROPERTY LEASE
Lease Reference HP-L-2026-0037

This Lease is made on March 9, 2026 between Harbour Point Estates Limited, a company registered in England and Wales with its registered office at 14 Dockside Row, Bristol BS1 5TY ("Landlord"), and Calder & Fenn Bookbinders Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales with its registered office at 9 Maltings Lane, Bath BA1 2QR ("Tenant").

1. Demised Premises. The Landlord lets to the Tenant the ground-floor workshop unit known as Unit 4, Harbour Point Industrial Estate, together with the loading bay immediately adjoining it and the right to use six marked parking spaces.

2. Term and Rent. The term is ten (10) years from the Commencement Date. The initial annual rent is forty-eight thousand pounds (GBP 48,000), payable quarterly in advance on the usual quarter days, subject to upward-only review on the fifth anniversary.

3. Rent Review. The reviewed rent is the open market rent of the Premises on the review date, assuming a willing landlord and willing tenant and a term equal to the residue of the term, disregarding any goodwill attributable to the Tenant's business.

4. Repair. The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good and substantial repair and condition, damage by insured risks excepted, and shall yield up the Premises in that condition at the end of the term.

5. Alterations. The Tenant shall not make structural alterations. Non-structural alterations require the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

6. Insurance. The Landlord shall insure the Premises against loss or damage by the insured risks and the Tenant shall reimburse the premium as additional rent within fourteen (14) days of demand.

7. Assignment and Subletting. The Tenant shall not assign, underlet or part with possession of part only of the Premises. Assignment of the whole requires the Landlord's consent under section 19(1A) of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1927.

8. Forfeiture. If the rent remains unpaid for twenty-one (21) days after becoming due, or if the Tenant breaches any other obligation, the Landlord may re-enter the Premises and the term shall absolutely determine.

9. Notices. Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand or sent by recorded delivery to the addresses above, or to such other address as a party notifies in writing.

10. Governing Law. This Lease and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
