SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Northwind Traders Ltd ("Customer") and
Contoso Services LLC ("Provider").

1. SERVICES. Provider shall supply the managed services described in the
applicable Statement of Work.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within
thirty (30) days of invoice receipt.

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Each party's aggregate liability under this
Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under the
applicable Statement of Work.

4. INDEMNIFICATION. Each party shall indemnify the other solely for
third-party claims arising from its gross negligence, willful misconduct, or
infringement of intellectual property under this Agreement.

5. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30)
days' prior written notice. All completed work shall be payable upon
termination.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Customer owns the deliverables created
specifically for this engagement. Provider retains ownership of its
pre-existing intellectual property, reusable tools and methodologies.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall protect the other party's proprietary
information using no less than reasonable care, and shall not disclose it to
third parties.

8. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware.
